Docs/Tutorial Kit/Features

Coach Marks and Hints

Every step shows in one of two styles. Set it on the flow (Style) and override it per step.

Coach marks #

For menus, mobile games and strategy games. The screen dims around a spotlight on the target, a pointer shows the way, and the tip card waits beside it. The Input Gate decides what the player can click:

  • Only Target: the target (and the card) only. Clicks inside the spotlight reach the real button.
  • Block All: only the card's buttons.
  • Allow All: nothing is blocked.

In-game hints #

For action games. A small tip that never dims, never blocks and never pauses play: "Press Space to jump", gone when the player jumps. Hints fit their words, sit at the bottom, top or centre of the screen (or beside their target), and can still ring their target. Set where they appear under the theme's In-game hints settings.

Use Show Until Learned on the flow for hints that come back until the player has done the thing a few times, and a Tutorial Trigger to show a hint the first time the player meets something.
