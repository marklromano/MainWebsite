Docs/Tutorial Kit/Features

Make It Yours

Everything the player sees and hears can be changed or switched off, without code.

Looks #

Pick a Theme Preset on the flow: Clean Dark, Minimal, Mobile Casual, Sci-Fi HUD or Fantasy Parchment. For your own, choose Assets > Create > All In One Tutorial Kit > Theme, set it on the flow's Theme field and change anything. Presets keep your own pictures, font and sounds when you switch.

Group Settings
Overlay Dim on or off, colour and strength.
Spotlight Shape (rounded rectangle, circle, oval, none), padding, corner radius, softness.
Outline On or off, colour, width, pulse.
Pointer On or off, colour, size, bob, your own picture.
Message Display As, card position, custom point and offset, default icon, icon colour and plate.
Tip card Colour, border, corner radius, width, padding, gap, font, heading and text colours and sizes, counter or progress dots, your own picture.
Buttons Colours, corner radius, height, text size, your own picture, Skip and Back on or off.
In-game hints Position, widest size, distance from the edge, ring on or off.
Sounds Step, click and finish sounds, volume.
Motion Entrance (none, fade, scale, slide), transition time, Reduce Motion.

Display As #

How much each card shows: Full (heading and text), Short Text, Icon Only, Icon And Short Text, or None (only the highlight; buttons still show when the step needs Next). Set it on the theme, override it per step.

Card position #

Auto picks the side with room; or Below, Above, Right, Left, Center, Screen Top, Screen Bottom, or Custom (a point on screen plus an offset). The card never leaves the screen or covers its target.

Per step #

Any step can have its own look (Theme Override), Display As, position, style and input gate.

Your own pictures #

Card, main button and pointer each take your sprite. Sprites with borders are 9-sliced; all are tinted by the part's colour.

Words #

Every word goes through TutorialText.Translate: set it to your localisation lookup, and you can write keys instead of sentences in steps.

Accessibility #

UI Scale on the presenter for larger text, Reduce Motion on the theme, high-contrast looks (Minimal), and real time timers that you can lengthen.
