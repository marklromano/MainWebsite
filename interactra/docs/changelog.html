Changelog

All notable changes to Interactra. Format: Keep a Changelog. Versions: Semantic Versioning.

[1.0.0] - Unreleased #

Added #

  • First release. Interactions: look, proximity, pointer and touch targeting; press, hold, tap, double tap, mash and toggle; locks and keys, power, use limits; doors, drawers, roll-ups, switches, valves, dials, keypads, carry and throw, sockets, inspect, readables, signs, audio logs, breakables, elevators, ladders, seats and screens; power wiring with logic, pressure plates and sequence puzzles; prompts with button pictures for keyboard and gamepads; themes; Setup Wizard, batch convert and the Workbench; optional multiplayer with Netcode for GameObjects.
  • Building: free, grid and socket placement; a preview that says why a spot is refused; build zones; move and remove; blueprints that fill in as Build is held; build animations (grow, rise, drop, assemble) and debris on remove; resource costs; a build menu; Make Buildable for your own models.
  • Wiring in play with wire looks (tidy, loose, straight, hidden); elevator doors and call buttons that light up; breakables that shatter; a throw arc for carried objects; player options; four looks for every screen; places for map products.
