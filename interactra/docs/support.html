Support

Need help? Every message gets an answer.

In Unity, Tools > Interactra > Help and Support lists every support address (Discord, bug reports, email) with Copy buttons, and Copy Support Info copies the Interactra version, Unity version, render pipeline, platform and input settings. Paste it in your message.

  • Discord: https://discord.gg/AmpBDxRAKu (ask in #help)
  • Email: raregeometrysupport@gmail.com
  • Bug reports: https://github.com/raregeometry/Support/issues
  • Website and online manual: https://raregeometry.github.io/interactra/

Before you write, two things solve most problems: - Press F3 in Play Mode: the debug overlay says why something can't be used. - Read Troubleshooting.

Enjoying Interactra? A short review on the Asset Store helps a lot.
