All In One Prefab Builder Kit

Your asset packs are full of great pieces. The Prefab Builder turns any of them into a kit you can build with: fast, snapped, and without ever changing the pack's own files. Everything happens in one window.

Get it on the Asset Store Quick start
  • Unity 6000.0 LTS+
  • Built-in, URP & HDRP
  • No dependencies
  • Full C# source

How it works

From a folder of prefabs to a city in three steps

  1. Add a pack

    A folder in your project, a .unitypackage, or a folder or .zip on disk. Prefabs and raw models both work.

  2. Check one review screen

    Every piece is measured and sorted for you: category, front, size, grid and floor type. Change what you like; your edits survive a re-scan.

  3. Build

    Pick a piece in the palette and click in the Scene view. R turns, T tilts, green fits, red overlaps.

Features

Seven tabs, one window

Each tab has a few modes and shows one at a time, so the window stays small. 1–9 pick tools, Shift+1–7 pick tabs, and every shortcut can be rebound.

Place

Stamp · Roads · Brush · Fill · Row · Spline
  • Stamp: pieces snap flush to their neighbours, line up and take their turn, or snap to the grid.
  • Roads: drag a road and the right tiles go down: straights, corners, T-junctions, crossroads, dead ends.
  • Brush: paint trees, rocks and clutter by radius and density, never on top of each other.
  • Fill, Row and Spline: fill an area or lay lines and curves of anything, with start and end caps.
  • Works on terrain, on roofs and tables, and inside Prefab Mode. N re-rolls the last fill with a new mix.

Dress

  • Street furniture along every road, by your rules
  • Road closures in one drag
  • Signs with your own text, decals, sagging cables
  • Fences with corners and a gate placed for you
  • Vary: swap lookalikes and colours so nothing looks copy-pasted

Physics

  • Drop or throw pieces and let real physics settle them
  • Scatter: spread, explode, heap or nudge
  • Arrange in a line, arc, circle or grid
  • Untangle pieces that are inside each other

Move

  • Move, rotate and scale with the kit's snapping
  • Select by a box, circle or ball you draw
  • Hide or lock a whole category while you work
  • Cutaway: slice off roofs and near walls to build inside

Fix

  • Snap to the ground
  • Find floating and sunken objects
  • Remove doubles, select similar
  • Randomize turn, tilt, scale and colour
  • Replace pieces and keep their place

Check

  • Find pieces clipping into each other
  • Validate the scene against your own rules
  • Performance against your budgets
  • Press Play from a clicked spot
  • Measure distances and walking time

Settings

Your rules, your keys
  • Nothing assumes a game. Categories, grids, budgets, floor types, styles and sign text are data you change, and export as a preset to share between projects.
  • Every shortcut can be rebound, and tabs, tools and palette go in the order you drag them.
  • Add your own tabs and tools from C#, and listen for what gets placed.

Good to know

Limits, stated up front

  • It's an editor tool: it builds scenes in the Unity Editor and adds nothing to your game at runtime beyond a few tiny helper components.
  • The cutaway shows one cut at a time and slices the Scene view only.

Updating

  • Delete the old product folder first, then import the new version. Your kits and settings live in a separate data folder and are kept.
