Docs/Hit Feedback Kit/Features

Accessibility

Built in, and meant for your game's options menu. Set the starting values in Project Settings > All In One Hit Feedback Kit (saved in Assets/HitFeedbackKitData/Resources), and change them while playing through HitFeedbackSettings:

Setting What it does
ReduceMotion No camera shake, punch or zoom kick, no global hitstop or slow motion, no knockback or squash. Numbers fade in place without moving or shaking. The low-health pulse glows steadily.
FlashSafety, MaxFlashesPerSecond An object flashes at full strength at most 3 times a second (common photosensitivity guidance); extra flashes are dimmed. Screen flashes closer than a third of a second apart are dimmed to a faint pulse. On by default.
ColorBlindSafe Numbers use each kind's colour-blind safe colour (based on the Okabe-Ito palette).
TextScale Numbers and bar text bigger or smaller (0.5 to 2.5).
Juice A master slider for every effect.
Volume, Rumble Hit sound volume; gamepad rumble on or off.
Enabled Turns all hit feedback off. 
HitFeedbackSettings.ReduceMotion = optionsMenu.reduceMotion;
HitFeedbackSettings.TextScale = optionsMenu.textSize;
HitFeedbackSettings.NotifyChanged();

Numbers are never the only signal: each kind also has its own colour, size and sound, and words (Miss, Blocked) say what happened.
