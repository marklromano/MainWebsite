Docs/Hit Feedback Kit/Features

Damage numbers

Numbers and words drawn on top of the finished frame (after post effects, so bloom never smears them), the same in every render pipeline. They are pooled and steady play makes no garbage.

Settings (Profile > Numbers) #

Setting What it does
Display as Number, Number And Icon, Icon, Word (the kind's word), None. Also per object under Overrides.
Motion Rise, Arc (pops up and falls to a side), Punch (pops big and settles), Slide, Stay.
Space Screen: always on top, the same size at any distance. World: sized by distance, and can hide behind walls.
Anchor Above the target (above its bar), at the hit point, or at its middle. Plus an offset.
Size Text height in pixels at 1080p; scales with the screen, each kind's size and the player's text size.
Font, Bold Any font asset in your project; empty uses Unity's built-in font. No fonts ship with the kit.
Outline, Shadow, Shine A clean outline, a drop shadow, and a top-to-bottom shine.
Lifetime, Distance, Spread, Pop How long, how far, how much sideways spread, how big the pop.
Combine window, Combine growth Hits within the window add up into one growing number that pops again on each hit.
Size by amount Bigger amounts show bigger numbers, up to Big Size at Big Amount.
Crit shake Critical and killing numbers shake for a moment.
Avoid overlap Numbers that would overlap push each other apart.
Follow target Numbers move with the target, or stay where they appeared.
Format, Decimals, Plus on heals 1234, 1,234 or 1.2K / 3.4M / 5.6B / 7.8T.
Hide behind walls, Wall layers World space only: a raycast from the camera every 0.1 s.
Max distance Numbers farther than this aren't shown.
Max on screen The oldest number makes way when the limit is reached.

Per kind (Profile > Kinds) #

Colour, colour-blind safe colour, word ("Miss", "Blocked"), suffix ("!" makes 123!), size, icon.

Numbers anywhere #

DamageNumbers.Show(position, 25, HitKind.Heal);            // a number, Action RPG style or a profile you pass
DamageNumbers.ShowText(position, "Level up!", HitKind.Status, myProfile);
DamageNumbers.Hidden = true;                              // hide them (for a cut scene)

Your TextMesh Pro font #

With TextMesh Pro in your project (it comes with Unity 6's UI package), add TextMesh Pro Numbers (Add Component > All In One Hit Feedback Kit) to any object in the scene and drag in your TMP font asset and, if you like, a material preset (outline, glow, gradient). While it is enabled every number is shown with TextMesh Pro in your font, in world space, facing the camera; kinds, colours, words, formatting and sizes still come from your profiles. Turn it off and the kit draws its own numbers again. In 1.0 it shows one number per hit (it doesn't add hits into one growing number).

Your own text (UI Toolkit, your prefab) #

Set DamageNumbers.CustomDisplay to your own object implementing IDamageNumberDisplay. The kit hands it every number with its text already worked out ("1.2K", "+25", "Miss"), its colour, size and icon. See Code.

When numbers are hidden #

The screen layer hides while the game is paused (Time.timeScale at 0 and not set by the kit's own hitstop) and when HitFeedbackOverlay.Visible is false, for example while your own menu is open. Numbers from a scene are cleared when a new scene loads on its own.
