Sound, particles and the kill moment

Sound (Profile > Sound, clips under Kinds) #

Each kind has its own clips; one is picked at random. Kinds without clips use the Normal kind's (except Miss and Status).

Setting What it does
Volume, Pitch min and max Random pitch for variety.
Combo pitch step Hits that combine into one number raise the pitch a little each time.
Cooldown The shortest time between two sounds from the same target, so rapid hits don't stack.
Spatial blend 0: the same everywhere. 1: from the target's position.
Output An Audio Mixer group.

Sounds play from a pool of 16 voices shared by every target; the master volume is HitFeedbackSettings.Volume.

Particles (Profile > Particles) #

The kit's own Sparks (bright, stretched, falling), a Puff (soft, rising), both, or none. Count, size, speed, colour (or each kind's colour). They play where the hit landed and fly back along the hit. Add your own prefab (any particle effect) and it is spawned at the hit and returned to a pool after its lifetime.

Kill moment (Profile > Kill moment) #

On the killing blow (HitInfo.IsKill, KillingHit, or Health reaching zero):

Setting What it does
Slow motion, Slow motion time The game slows for a moment (safe: see Hitstop).
Number size The final number is bigger.
Extra shake, Extra hitstop A harder last hit.
Screen flash, colour A quick full-screen flash (dimmed by flash safety when kills come fast).
Pop and fade, Pop time The target pops up and shrinks away.
After, After delay Nothing (your code takes over), Disable or Destroy the object.

Pooling enemies? A popped object comes back at its own size when you enable it again or call health.Revive().
