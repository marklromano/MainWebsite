Troubleshooting and FAQ

First, open Why No Feedback? It names the reason for almost everything below, with a fix.

Nothing happens when I hit #

  • Is there a Hit Feedback on the object, or a parent of the collider you hit? Why No Feedback? catches hits on objects without one.
  • Using the kit's Health? Damage while invulnerable, or after death, plays nothing.
  • Is HitFeedbackSettings.Enabled on, and the master Juice above 0?

No numbers or no shake #

The kit needs a camera: the one tagged MainCamera, or set HitFeedbackSettings.Camera (or the camera tag in Project Settings). Numbers farther than Max Distance aren't shown.

Numbers show over my pause menu / under my UI #

The screen layer is drawn after the frame. It hides by itself while the game is paused (Time.timeScale at 0). For other menus set HitFeedbackOverlay.Visible = false while they are open. Your own OnGUI windows draw on top of it.

The flash doesn't show #

  • Static batched objects can't flash: untick Batching Static (Why No Feedback? has the button).
  • Particle, trail and line renderers aren't flashed.
  • Added renderers at runtime? Call RebuildFlash().

Knockback does nothing #

The object needs something to move: a non-kinematic Rigidbody or Rigidbody 2D, a Character Controller, a NavMesh Agent, or Move Transform turned on. Kinematic bodies are moved like transforms.

My camera controller and the shake #

They don't fight: the shake is added after your controller runs and removed before it runs again. If you shake a camera rig instead, add Hit Camera Shake to the camera and set its Shake Target.

Squash changes my collider #

Set Hit Feedback > Visual to a child that holds only the model.

Slow motion stayed on #

It can't: the game's own time scale always comes back, and if your game changes Time.timeScale (a pause menu) the kit lets go at once. TimeControl.StopAll() ends every stop now.

Is it multiplayer ready? #

Feedback is local; play it on each client from your networked hit event. See Code.

Known limits #

  • Numbers and bars are a screen layer, not 3D objects in the scene; "World" numbers are sized by distance and hidden behind walls by a raycast.
  • No UI Toolkit or VR-specific feedback in 1.0 (bring your own display through IDamageNumberDisplay).
