Docs/Hit Feedback Kit/Start here

All In One Hit Feedback Kit documentation

Make every hit land: damage numbers, hit flash, hitstop, screen shake, knockback, health bars and sound from one component, with genre presets and a Why No Feedback? debugger.

  • Unity 6000.0+
  • Built-in, URP & HDRP
  • No dependencies
  • Full C# source
  • Version 1.0.0
Get started

Quick start: five minutes

  1. Import the package. Everything lands in Assets/HitFeedbackKit/. Try Tools > All In One Hit Feedback Kit > Open Demo > Showcase and press Play.
  2. Select your enemies and open Tools > All In One Hit Feedback Kit > Setup Wizard.
  3. Pick a preset and click Add Hit Feedback.
  4. Hit them with HitFeedback.Hit(enemy, 25), the Health component or a UnityEvent. Nothing? Why No Feedback? tells you why, with a fix button.
