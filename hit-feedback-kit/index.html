All In One Hit Feedback Kit

Make every hit land. Add one component to any enemy, player or prop, and every hit plays the whole reaction: a damage number, a hit flash, hitstop, camera shake, knockback, squash, a health bar, a sound and sparks. Pick a genre preset for the right feel in one click. When something doesn't play, the debugger tells you why.

Read the documentation Join the Discord Features
  • Unity 6000.0+
  • Built-in, URP & HDRP
  • 2D and 3D
  • No dependencies

How it works

Hits that feel good, in three steps

  1. Add Hit Feedback

    Select your enemies, or a whole folder of prefabs, and open the Setup Wizard. Prefabs get a variant; your originals are never changed.

  2. Pick a preset

    Arcade, Action RPG, Souls-like, Cartoon, Shooter, Idle and Mobile or Minimal, then one Juice slider for more or less of everything.

  3. Hit them

    HitFeedback.Hit(enemy, 25), the kit's Health component, or a UnityEvent. Nothing happens? Why No Feedback? says why, with a fix.

Features

The whole hit, working together

Damage numbers

The headline
  • Every kind: normal, critical, heal, shield, miss, block, damage over time and status words, each with its own colour, size and sound.
  • Readable in a crowd: rapid hits add up into one growing number, numbers push apart instead of stacking, crits pop and shake.
  • 1.2K / 3.4M formats, icons, five motions, your own font, outline and shadow. Drawn after post effects, pooled, no garbage.

Hit flash

  • Meshes, skinned meshes and sprites
  • Whatever their material; yours are never changed
  • Built-in, URP and HDRP
  • Fill, rim, kind colours, invulnerability blink

Hitstop and shake

  • Freeze the target and its attacker, or the whole game
  • Stops never stack or get stuck
  • Shake and punch that never fight your camera controller; Cinemachine impulse option

Knockback and squash

  • Rigidbody, Rigidbody 2D, Character Controller, NavMesh Agent or transform
  • Scale punch, squash and stretch, wobble

Health bars

  • Floating bars with delayed drain, shield and segments
  • A boss bar and a player HUD bar

The player's side

  • Damage vignette and direction arrows
  • Low-health pulse and gamepad rumble

Works with your game

No code
  • The Setup Wizard finds your own health script and connects it with a Hit Feedback Bridge
  • It watches your health value, or your UnityEvents call it; it never changes your health
  • Recipes for projectiles, hitboxes and hitscan; a safe Existing Game preset

Why No Feedback?

  • Every part of every hit: played or skipped, and why
  • One-click fixes; Scene Overview

Make it yours, and accessible

No code
  • Eight presets (seven genre feels and a safe Existing Game preset), then change any value; per-object overrides; a live preview of your numbers in the inspector.
  • Accessibility built in: Reduce Motion, flash safety, colour-blind safe colours and text size, ready for your options menu.

Good to know

Limits, stated up front

  • The kit shows hits; it doesn't decide them. No weapons, AI or stats (the optional Health covers the basics; recipe components for projectiles, hitboxes and hitscan are included).
  • Feedback is local: play it on each client from your own networked hit event.
  • The kit's own numbers and bars are a screen layer; an optional TextMesh Pro display puts numbers in the scene. No UI Toolkit or VR-specific feedback in 1.0.
