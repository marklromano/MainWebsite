How it works

Place it. Connect it. Use it.

  1. Make it interactive

    The Setup Wizard turns your own prefab into a door, drawer, switch or pickup in a few steps, with the prompt already working.

  2. Let players build

    Players place pieces on sockets and a grid, with a ghost that says why a spot is refused, blueprints to finish and debris when they knock something down.

  3. Wire it up

    Levers, buttons and plates power doors and lamps. Players wire them in play, along the walls, tidy or loose, and the whole build saves and loads.

Features

Everything in the box

Your own prefabs

Setup Wizard

Pick a prefab, pick what it does, and it works: doors, drawers, lids, switches, pickups, seats, ladders and more, with no code.

Doors that do more

Hinged, sliding, roll-up and physics doors; tap to open, hold to lock; keys, keypads, combination dials and powered doors that open from a lever.

Runtime building

Ghosts and sockets

Place, move, paint, upgrade and copy pieces. Snap to sockets or a grid, reach into the ground on slopes, and see the reason when a spot is refused.

Blueprints and build feel

Place a blueprint and hold Build to finish it. Pieces grow, rise, drop or assemble into place, and break into debris when removed.

Power and wiring

Levers, buttons and pressure plates power doors, lamps and gates. Players wire them in play; wires run along walls, hang loose or hide.

Hands-on objects

Carry and throw with a landing arc, valves to turn or drag, crates that break apart, elevators with sliding doors and call buttons that light up, notes, signs and inspectable items.

Make it yours

Four looks

Clean, Industrial, Fantasy and Sci-Fi looks for every prompt and screen, or your own colours, fonts and sounds.

Players have options

Prompt size, press instead of hold, captions, reduced motion and wire visibility, chosen by your players within the rules you set.

Easy to own

Workbench, saving, multiplayer

One Workbench window to set up, inspect and test; a debugger that says why something can't be used; save and load; optional multiplayer with Netcode for GameObjects.
