How it works

Connect the game you already built

  1. Open the Launch Desk

    Tools > Shipready > Launch Desk. Pick your game's starting point. Shipready shows every step before anything changes.

  2. Connect

    Menus, settings and saves join your scenes. Your Event System, canvases, cameras and scripts are left as they are.

  3. Press Play

    The title screen opens, settings apply and come back, and the game saves and continues where the player left off.

Features

Everything players touch before, between and after playing

Save. Quit. Continue.

Saves
  • Slots, autosaves and quicksaves that survive crashes, with a picture, place, chapter and play time on every card.
  • Objects remember their state with one component; the world loads back exactly as the player left it.
  • Six save menu styles, or your own colours, words and layout. Backups and automatic recovery from damaged saves.

Player settings

  • 92 ready-made settings: display, graphics, audio, controls and accessibility
  • Key and button rebinding for keyboard, mouse and gamepad
  • Display changes that undo themselves unless kept

Accessible

  • Five accessibility presets
  • Text and menu size, high contrast, reduced motion
  • Subtitles with speaker names and sound captions

One look, every screen

  • Six Game Look presets, or your own colours and fonts
  • Menus, settings, saves, tours and reports all follow it
  • Readable by design: every text colour is checked

A complete front end

  • Title, main menu, pause, confirmation, loading and credits screens
  • Mouse, keyboard and gamepad everywhere
  • Continue loads the latest save

Help players find their way

  • Coach marks, hints and step-by-step tours
  • They point at the real button and finish when the player does the thing
  • Skip and Next built in

Playtest with context

  • Testers press one key: their words, a screenshot and their recent steps in one report
  • A Report Inbox in the editor and a debug overlay with your own buttons
  • Reports to Discord, Slack or your server

Catch problems before players do

Readiness
  • One click checks your build list, names, input and every part of Shipready, and says what to fix first.
  • Fixes in one click: remove a missing scene or open the right setting straight from the result.
  • Player Options: one asset decides which settings, saves and tester tools each build allows.

Technical details

Built to be dependable

Unity6000.0.85f1 or newer (tested on 6000.0, 6000.3 and 6000.6)
Render pipelinesBuilt-in, URP and HDRP
InputInput Manager (old) or the Input System package
PlatformsTested on Windows and in web (WebGL) builds
NeedsUnity UI (uGUI) with TextMesh Pro, which every Unity 6 template has
SavesJSON files with backups and crash recovery; browser storage on the web
SourceFull C#, one assembly per part, so unused parts can be removed
MenuTools > Shipready

Good to know

Limits, stated up front

  • Screen readers are not supported: Unity UI has no screen reader support.
  • Android and iOS haven't been tested yet.
  • Your data stays in your own folder, so updates never touch it.

Status
