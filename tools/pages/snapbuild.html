How it works

From a modular pack to a street in three steps

  1. Add your pack

    A folder in your project, a folder on disk or a .zip. Every piece is measured and sorted for you, and Snapbuild guesses which pieces are walls, corners, floors and roofs. Check one review screen.

  2. Drag rooms and roads

    Build > Rooms: drag a rectangle or click any outline and a whole building goes down. Build > Roads: drag along the grid and the right tiles join up.

  3. Dress and check

    Dress the street with lights, signs and fences, Arrange props with physics, then let Check find anything clipping or floating.

Features

Five tabs, one window

Everything lives in the Level Builder, in the order you build: Kit, Build, Dress, Arrange, Check. Every control has a tooltip, every action is one Undo, and every shortcut can be rebound.

Build

Rooms · Stamp · Roads · Brush · Fill · Row · Spline
  • Rooms: whole buildings from one drag: walls, corners, floors, roof, door and windows. Rooms stay rooms: resize them, add stories, make any wall a window, door, gap or steps, add stairs, save blueprints.
  • Roads: straights, corners, T-junctions and crossroads picked and joined as you draw. Shift-drag takes a road up.
  • Brush, Fill, Row and Spline: paint woods, tile an area, lay rows, paths and fences along your lines.
  • Build effect: new pieces rise, drop or pop into place.

Kit

  • Add any modular pack: prefabs, FBX, OBJ, .zip
  • Roles: which piece is a wall, corner or roof
  • Build Catalog for in-game build menus

Dress

  • Street furniture down both sides of a road
  • Signs with your own text, road markings
  • Power lines and bunting that sag
  • Fences, road closures, Vary

Arrange

  • Drop and throw with real physics
  • Line, arc, circle and grid
  • Mirror, extrude and radial copies
  • Move, rotate and scale with snapping

Check

  • Clipping, floating, sunken, doubles
  • Validate against your rules, measure
  • Level ground, combine meshes
  • Play from here; playable marks

Good to know

Updating from the Prefab Builder Kit 1.0

  • Snapbuild is a free update on the same Asset Store page. Your kits, rule sets, palettes, presets, scenes and keys carry over.
  • Your data lives outside the product folder, so updates never touch it.

Limits, stated up front

  • Rooms build on a grid with square corners: any L, T or courtyard outline works, curved walls don't.
  • It's an editor tool: it adds nothing to your game at runtime beyond a few small helper components.
