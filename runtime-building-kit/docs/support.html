Support

  • Discord: https://discord.gg/AmpBDxRAKu (questions, help, and what's coming)
  • Bug tracker: https://github.com/marklromano/RomanoAssetSupport (report a bug or ask for a feature; choose "All In One Runtime Building Kit" in the form)
  • Online documentation: https://marklromano.github.io/runtime-building-kit/docs/
  • Website: https://marklromano.github.io (product page)
  • Email: marklromano@outlook.com (for anything you'd rather not post publicly)

When you report a problem, please paste the kit's support info: open the kit window, Help tab > Copy support info (kit version, Unity version, render pipeline, platform, input system), and say what you did, what you expected and what happened.

Known problems are listed in Known Issues.

If this kit helps you, a review on the Asset Store helps a lot. Thank you!
