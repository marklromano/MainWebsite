Changelog

All notable changes to Snapbuild (called the Prefab Builder kit up to 1.0). Versions follow Semantic Versioning.

[2.0.0] - Unreleased #

A free update for everyone who owns 1.0. The product has a new name, a new window and a new flagship: whole rooms built from your kit's pieces. What you made with 1.0 keeps working: kits, rule sets, palettes, presets, scenes and the keys you set.

Added #

  • Building ghost: while you drag in Rooms, a see-through ghost of the whole building shows every piece where it will stand.
  • Build effect: new pieces rise out of the ground, drop or pop into place in the Scene view (or None). In Rooms and Roads, for every tool.
  • Clearer ghosts: every ghost is lit with a bright edge so their shape reads, in Snapbuild's ghost blue (red where something is in the way).
  • Rooms (Build): drag a rectangle and a room goes down: walls, corners, a floor, a roof, a door and windows, from the kit's building parts. Draw any outline with square corners, or a run of walls. Stories stack.
  • Rooms stay rooms: select one and drag a side to resize it; click a wall to make it a window, a door, steps or a gap; Next variant, Freeze, Detach, Re-roll, Rebuild, Bake. Only what changed is rebuilt, and what you chose stays put.
  • Stairs: a staircase on any floor tile of a room, with the floor above left open.
  • Blueprints: save a room and build it again anywhere; R turns it.
  • Roles (Kit): what each piece is in a building, its family and how often it is used, guessed from names, sizes and the shape of the models, and yours to correct.
  • Repeat (Arrange): mirror, extrude and radial copies of the selection, shown before they are made. Left and right pieces are swapped for their twins; a mirrored room is still a room.
  • Level ground (Check): flattens a Unity Terrain under what is placed and clears the trees and grass there, with a blend back into the hills. As I place does it for every piece stamped.
  • Combine (Check): the selection as one mesh per material, with the pieces kept so Un-combine brings them back.
  • Playable (Check): stable IDs, places for maps and compasses, shared surfaces for footsteps, marks on doors and windows. Each works alone, and the other Rare Geometry tools pick them up when installed. Marks show in the Scene view: a pin with its name on each place, a tag on each door, window and stairs.
  • Where pieces may land: one filter for the Brush and Fill: slope, height, layers and a tag.
  • Fill: the whole area, a border along its edge, or everything but the border.
  • Fences: a fence dragged as a rectangle can be resized by its sides.
  • Roads: Shift + drag along a road takes it up and the roads beside it close up again (a junction that lost its side road becomes a straight). Side by side: one drag lays two or three roads, a set gap apart, turning together.
  • Stamp: the ghost says its size, shows its snap points and names what it overlaps.
  • Inside roof corners: an L-shaped room's roof gets the kit's inside-corner roof piece where its edges meet (a new role, Roof inside corner, in Kit > Roles), turned the right way from its shape.
  • Build catalog (Kit > Catalog): a kit's pieces as a catalog for a build menu, yours or a building product's: each piece with a name, a picture, a category, its size, its grid and what it is. Names, pictures, categories and which pieces are offered are yours to set on the catalog, and are kept when it is made again.
  • Signs and decals at run time: change a sign's text and colour from your game, translate signs by key, and swap, tint, fade, resize or hide a decal, in builds too.

Changed #

  • Snapbuild and the Level Builder: the new names, with a header (help, settings behind the gear, a status pill) and five tabs (Kit, Build, Dress, Arrange, Check) that fit a narrow docked window.
  • Menus and shortcuts: everything is under Tools > Snapbuild and Edit > Shortcuts > Snapbuild. Keys you changed in 1.0 are carried over once.
  • Folders: new projects get Assets/RareGeometry/Snapbuild and keep kits in Assets/RareGeometryData/Snapbuild. A project updated from 1.0 keeps its folders until you move them from the update panel.
  • Kits moved from Settings to the Kit tab.

Fixed #

  • Clipping (Check) no longer lists a room's own windows, walls and roof against each other: they meet by design. A room against anything else is still checked.
  • Floating (Check) checks a room as one building: its roof rests on its walls, and the room is checked against the ground under it.
  • Cables (Dress): Hang from the top of poles now finds the top of a pole with a cross arm, such as a telegraph pole.
  • Roofs (Rooms): a roof's corners are only picked from the pieces that meet the roof edges beside them, so a slanted roof no longer shows a gap at its corners. The pack's other corner pieces stay available through Next variant.
  • Vary (Dress): the panel no longer reports a layout error when the selection changes while it is open.

[1.0.0] - 2026-10-27 #

A free update for everyone who owns 0.8: easier to start, clearer to use, safer in your project. Nothing you made with 0.8 changes: kits, rule sets, palettes, hotkeys and scenes all carry over.

Added #

  • Home (⌂): try the sample city, turn your pack into a kit, take the tour, and eight jobs (lay roads, build a street, plant trees and grass, dress a street, fence a yard, close a road, pile up clutter, check the scene), each opening the right tool, ready for the job.
  • Tool presets: Brush, Fill, Row, Spline and Fence keep their settings and picked pieces by name ("Grass brush"), Presets ▾ on the Settings card.
  • Status line: a line at the foot of the Scene view says what a click does in the tool that's open, and its keys.
  • Get help, report a bug: the guide, the public bug list, the website, Discord and email as addresses to copy, and Copy support info: from ⋮ or the Tools menu.
  • Sample decals: road arrows, a manhole cover and an oil stain (CC0) to try Dress > Decals on straight away.
  • A longer tour: it goes on to Roads, Physics and Check, and moves on by itself when a step is done.

Changed #

  • Unity 6000.0 LTS: the kit now runs on Unity 6000.0 LTS or newer (it needed 6000.3).
  • The sample kits are ready to use: Street's lights and bins and the closure's barriers and cones are filled in from them, and adding them shows a progress bar. Sample scenes open looking at the city.
  • Fits a docked window: the tabs and tools wrap onto a second row instead of scrolling out of sight; ? and ⋮ are always whole; settings are never cut in half.
  • Plain-English Settings: Rules and Folders in plain words with units, the rarely changed ones under Advanced; the optional layer and tags no longer show warnings.
  • Clearer names: Try the sample kits everywhere; Fix > Similar and Physics > Drop (they shared names with other tools); Allow overlaps (was Place over red), Same floor type only (Stay on this floor), Ground pieces sit on top (Floors on top of the ground), Whole panels only (Fixed), Panels face in (Fronts in), Around the selection (Inverse), Facing (Turn them).
  • Readable palette names: long names keep their start and end ("comm…building-a"), the full name on hover.
  • Vary: pieces varied together no longer come out the same colour.

Fixed #

  • Your project's other files are never saved by the kit: adding the sample kits could save an unrelated settings file (a URP asset) along with them.
  • Tool picks stay in their project: picks (brush, slots, signs, recent decals…) were shared by every project on the PC.
  • Sign text sits on the board: text goes on the sign's board whichever way it faces, never on its pole beside it, its plain back, or behind a bar across it (the sample street and highway signs); a bare post gets a sign-shaped board at its top.
  • Running the kit's tests leaves your settings alone: some tests could leave the scene folder and data folder pointing at their own test folders.
  • Undo keeps the files right: undoing a change to a rule set (a Street kind, say) now puts the file back too.
  • Home is Home: opening it puts the tool away, so no tab, tool, overlay or status line says a tool is in use while it isn't (the overlay says Home is open).
  • Nothing cut off in a docked window: Fence's three options, Spline's "m apart", the Help window's addresses and long piece names in a kit's review fit (names keep their start and end). Tool rows take the room their names need, so a tab of seven tools no longer wraps at the usual docked width.
  • The Scene status line stays in sight above a toolbar docked at the foot of the Scene view, and wraps in a narrow view.
  • Clearer Scene labels: the Brush's count sits beside its circle and the road's cell count is readable, both on a dark backing.
  • Roads shows road tiles: the palette shows only road tiles while Roads is open, and goes back to what it showed after.
  • Closures and other tools with many settings use more of a tall window before their settings scroll.
  • Vary: Space works with the window in focus, and its settings no longer move when the selection changes.
  • Check > Performance: the heavy mesh you clicked stays lit in the list.
  • Updating a kit with no style (the sample kits) no longer makes a new style for it: Style shows None.
  • What a tool built (a closure, fence, spline, cable) no longer shows "Not in a kit" when selected.
  • Street furniture stands off the road: a kind's distance from the road edge is measured to the near side of what stands on the ground (a bin's side, a light's pole), so deep pieces no longer hang half over the kerb, and lights stand by the kerb with their arms over the road. Dressed roads show their furniture instead of red ghosts over it.

Earlier versions #

Versions 0.1 to 0.8 (2026) were the first releases, under the product's earlier names. Their changes are part of 1.0.
