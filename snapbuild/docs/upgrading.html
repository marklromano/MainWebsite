Upgrading

How to update Snapbuild, and what changes for you in each version. The full list of changes is in CHANGELOG.md, in the product folder.

How to update #

  1. Save your scene.
  2. Import the new package from the Package Manager (My Assets), over the version you have.

That is all. Unity updates every file where it already is, so nothing in your scenes changes. Your own work isn't in the product folder: your kits, rule sets, palettes, blueprints and placeable copies are in your data folder, the project's settings in ProjectSettings/AllInOneWorldBuilderSettings.asset (the file keeps its first name, so your settings are found), and your hotkeys and tool presets in Unity's own preferences. Pieces you placed are ordinary objects in your scenes.

If you prefer a clean install, delete the product folder first and then import: the result is the same, with the product in its new folder straight away.

2.0.0 #

A free update for everyone who owns 1.0 (the Prefab Builder kit). The product is called Snapbuild now and its window the Level Builder. Nothing you made changes: kits, rule sets, palettes, presets, scenes and placed pieces carry over, and every public C# type and member of 1.0 is still there (a test checks this).

What is different for you:

  • Menus: everything is under Tools > Snapbuild. The window opens with Tools > Snapbuild > Level Builder (Alt+Shift+W, as before).
  • Five tabs: Kit, Build, Dress, Arrange and Check. Build holds what was Place; Arrange holds Move and Physics; Check holds Fix and Check; Kits moved from Settings to the Kit tab; the rest of Settings is behind the gear. The tools are the same and keep their keys. Shift+1 to 5 pick the tabs, Shift+6 Settings, Shift+7 Home.
  • Shortcuts: they are listed under Snapbuild in Edit > Shortcuts. Keys you changed in 1.0 are carried over the first time the new version loads (you will see a line in the Console saying how many).
  • Folders: after an update the files are where they were (Assets/AllInOnePrefabBuilderKit, and your data in Assets/AllInOnePrefabBuilderData). Everything works there. The Level Builder shows a panel with two buttons, to use when it suits you: Move Snapbuild to Assets/RareGeometry/Snapbuild and Move your data to Assets/RareGeometryData/Snapbuild. Both keep every file's ID, so scenes, kits and prefabs carry on as they were. The ✕ puts the panel away for good.
  • A shared folder: Assets/RareGeometry/Core and Assets/RareGeometry/EditorUI are new. They are small and shared by every Rare Geometry product, so two products never bring two copies. Leave them where they are.
  • Colours: the window's highlight is yellow now (it was blue). Ghosts and snap lines are blue.
  • Your own tools: a tool you wrote for 1.0 (a WorldBuilderMode subclass) still shows. One that named a tab of its own now shows among the Build tools.

New in 2.0, all optional: Rooms and Roles, Repeat, Level ground, Combine, the placement filter, Fill's border and middle, fence resizing and Make Playable. See the overview.

1.0.0 #

A free update for everyone who owns 0.8. Nothing to do: kits, rule sets, palettes, hotkeys and scenes made with 0.8 all carry over, and every menu item, hotkey and public C# type and member of 0.8 is still there (a test checks this).

  • Unity 6000.0 LTS or newer (0.8 needed 6000.3).
  • Home (⌂) opens the first time for someone with no kits yet. You can open it any time from the ⌂ button.
  • Some tools and options have clearer names: Check > Similar and Arrange > Drop (they shared names with other tools), Allow overlaps (was Place over red), Same floor type only (Stay on this floor), Ground pieces sit on top (Floors on top of the ground), Whole panels only (Fixed), Panels face in (Fronts in), Around the selection (Inverse), Facing (Turn them). They do what they did; your settings for them are kept.
  • Settings show in plain words with units; the rarely changed ones are under Advanced.
  • Tool picks are kept per project now. Picks you made in 0.8 (brush pieces, slots, recent decals) start empty once in each project; pick them again.
  • Sign text finds the board more reliably. Signs placed with 0.8 keep their text where it is until you change them.

0.8.0 #

  • The product folder and the data folder were renamed (an old data folder is renamed for you the first time the kit loads, so your kits keep working).
  • Needed Unity 6000.3 or newer.
