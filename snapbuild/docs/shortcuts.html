Keyboard shortcuts

Every Snapbuild shortcut with its default keys. They are Unity shortcuts, so you can change any of them in Edit > Shortcuts (or the kit's own Settings > Hotkeys), and the tools follow your keys. Tool keys work while the mouse is over the Scene view or the kit's window.

Action Keys

Toggle Snapping Alt+Shift+L
Snap Selection Alt+Shift+G

Select Similar Alt+Shift+S

Mouse #

Action How
Stamp: place the piece Click
Stamp: place even where the ghost is red Shift + Click
Stamp: place freely (no snapping) Hold Ctrl (Cmd)
Stamp, Row, Signs, Decals, Physics stamp: turn freely, any angle Hold R and move the mouse sideways
Stamp, Physics stamp: tilt freely (its front down or up) Hold T and move the mouse sideways
Pick up a placed piece (eyedropper) Alt + Click
Drop a piece from the palette Drag it into the Scene view
Brush: paint / erase Drag / Shift + Drag
Brush: stay on the floor you start on Hold Ctrl (Cmd) while painting
Row: lay a row (any angle) Drag (hold Ctrl)
Spline: add a point / close the loop Click / Click the first point
Spline (editing): move / add / remove a point Drag a point / Click / Ctrl + Click a point
Fill, Fence: the area Drag a rectangle
Erase: erase the kit pieces under the circle Drag
Dress > Street: dress the roads / select Drag a rectangle over roads / Click
Dress > Closures: close a road / its end under the mouse / turn it round Drag a line across it / Click / R
Dress > Signs: place a sign / turn it Click / R
Dress > Vary: a new look for a piece / for the selection Click / Space
Dress > Decals: lay the image / turn it Click / R
Arrange > Select: box, circle or ball / add / take out / where both meet Drag (a box: then its height, click) / Shift / Ctrl / Shift + Ctrl
Arrange > Select: what's on screen, or the last shape again Space
Arrange > Drop: drop / a box, line or ring Click or Space / Drag
Arrange > Scatter: scatter the selection / explode from a spot Space / Click
Arrange > Arrange: start drawing, then draw where they go / by their own shape Space, then Drag or Click / Space twice
Arrange > Arrange: untangle the selection U
