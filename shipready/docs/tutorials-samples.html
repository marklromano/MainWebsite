Docs/Shipready/Tutorials

Samples

Open each from Tools > Shipready > Tutorials > Open Demo and press Play.

Demo Shows
Menu Tour (uGUI) A mobile-style menu, a coach-mark tour with spotlights, a click on the real Play button, and a Make it yours button that switches the five looks.
First Person Basics (3D hints) In-game hints that never pause play: walk (W A S D), look at the tower, jump, reach the glowing pad, collect 3 coins. Button pictures follow your device.
Platformer Hints (2D) Hints on 2D colliders with an orthographic camera: run, jump onto the ledge, coins, reach the flag.
Strategy Tour (UI Toolkit) A coach-mark tour of a UI Toolkit HUD: resources, build a farm, the minimap, end the turn.

The demo scripts (controllers, coins, the HUD) are only for the demos; your own controllers and UI work the same way.
