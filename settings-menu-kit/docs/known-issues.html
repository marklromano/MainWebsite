Known issues and limits

  • Screen readers (Narrator, NVDA, VoiceOver, TalkBack) are not supported: Unity's uGUI has no screen reader support.
  • HDRP: the menus and all engine-wide settings work; HDRP-specific graphics settings are not included in 1.0.
  • Right-to-left languages (Arabic, Hebrew) are not handled; Chinese, Japanese and Korean need a font asset that covers them (Languages).
  • Monitor selection is available on Windows only (Unity's API).
  • Mobile and consoles: the menus are safe-area aware and controller-friendly, but 1.0 was tested on Windows (Mono and IL2CPP players) and with a WebGL script build; Android and iOS builds were not tested.
  • Generic controllers show text labels for face buttons (they have no standard markings); Xbox, PlayStation and Switch controllers show pictures.

Report anything else on the bug tracker: https://github.com/romanoassets/RomanoAssetSupport
