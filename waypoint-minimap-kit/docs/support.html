Support

Need help? Every message gets an answer.

  • Documentation: https://raregeometry.github.io/waypoint-minimap-kit/docs/
  • Discord: https://discord.gg/AmpBDxRAKu (ask in #help)
  • Report a bug: https://github.com/raregeometry/Support/issues
  • Website: https://raregeometry.github.io/waypoint-minimap-kit/
  • Email: marklromano@outlook.com

In Unity: Tools > All In One Waypoint & Minimap Kit > Help and Support has these addresses with Copy buttons, and Copy Support Info copies the kit version, Unity version, render pipeline and platform. Paste it in your message.

Enjoying the kit? A short review on the Asset Store helps a lot.
