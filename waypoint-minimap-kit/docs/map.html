Maps: minimap and full-screen map

A Waypoint Map is a picture of your level from above. The radar shows it as a minimap, and M opens it as a full-screen map with the player and every waypoint on it. Waypoints and the player move live on the map; the picture itself is made once, in the editor (it is not a live camera view).

Add one #

  • Setup Wizard step 3: Add Map And Make It, or
  • GameObject > All In One Waypoint & Minimap Kit > Waypoint Map, then Fit To Scene and Make Clean Map.

The blue box in the Scene view is the area the map covers. Its top edge is north (the world's forward, Z).

Three kinds of picture #

Picture from What it is Best for
Clean map (drawn from the level) A clean, flat map drawn from your level's shapes: roads, paths, grass, sand, water, rock, buildings and forest, with relief shading and outlines. No camera, so it is the same in Built-in, URP and HDRP. Most 3D games
Photo from above A picture taken by a camera looking straight down. 2D games, or levels the Clean map can't read
My own picture Any top-down picture you painted, north at the top. Hand-made treasure maps, stylised games

Making a Clean map #

  1. Press Preview. The map is drawn in the inspector without saving anything.
  2. Anything shown in pink wasn't recognised. It's listed under the preview: pick what each one is (Road, Building, Water, Leave off the map...) and press Use. A rule is added and the preview is drawn again.
  3. Press Make Clean Map. The picture is saved in Assets/WaypointKitData/Maps.

How it decides what things are, first match wins:

  1. A Waypoint Map Category component on the object or a parent (add it to anything to say exactly what it is).
  2. Your rules (Colours, rules and finishing): words in a material name, object name, layer, tag or Terrain layer.
  3. The built-in words: road, street, asphalt; path, pavement, plaza, bridge; grass, lawn, field; sand, beach; water, sea, lake, river; rock, cliff, mountain; house, building, shop, tower, tent; tree, forest, pine...
  4. Small things (under 1.5 m across) are left off: props, crates, plants, characters.
  5. A hint from the material's colour (green is grass, blue is water).

Terrains are drawn from their Terrain layers (named like grass, sand, road, rock...) and their painted trees become forest. Height range draws one floor of a multi-level place.

Setting What it does
Picture size 64 to 4096 pixels on the longer side. Bigger is sharper but uses more memory; 1024 or 2048 is usually plenty.
Layers to draw Leave out the player, effects and UI.
Unknown things become Left off (default), or drawn as ground.
Relief, outlines Slope shading lit from the north-west; edges round buildings, roads and water.
Colours One colour per kind of place, and the empty colour (sea, void).

The full-screen map #

M (or hud.ToggleMap()) opens it. Drag to move, wheel or + / - to zoom, Me or Space to centre on the player, M or Escape to close. It shows the player arrow, every waypoint with its name, discovered region names, fog of war and a legend for Clean maps. On the HUD you choose the key, whether the mouse is freed (default) and whether the game pauses while it's open. Its look (colours, sizes, words) is in the style's Full-screen map section.

Gamepads: call hud.ToggleMap(), hud.ZoomMap(factor) and hud.PanMap(delta) from your own input.

Why not a live camera map? #

A live minimap renders the level a second time every frame through an extra camera, which works differently in each render pipeline and costs performance. A made picture looks the same everywhere, costs nothing, and never breaks with a Unity or pipeline update. If your level changes a lot while playing, make a few pictures and swap WaypointMap.Picture.
