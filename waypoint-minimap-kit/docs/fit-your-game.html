Fit it into your game

How the kit behaves in a real project, with your own controller, cameras, UI, quests and saves. Each case says what the kit does by itself and what (if anything) you do.

Your player and camera #

Your setup What happens What you do
First person (camera inside the player) Distances from the player Nothing
Third person (camera behind the player) Distances and reaching are measured from the object tagged Player, found by itself (again after a respawn) Tag your character Player, or set the HUD's Player
No Player tag Distances come from the camera; the HUD inspector and the scene check say so Set Player for third person
Cinemachine Cinemachine drives one real Camera (with the Cinemachine Brain); the HUD uses that camera Nothing (or set View Camera to the Brain's camera)
Switching cameras (vehicles, cameras made at runtime) With View Camera empty, the HUD uses whatever camera is tagged MainCamera, every frame Nothing, or call hud.SetCamera(cam)
Changing characters hud.SetPlayer(newCharacter)
Cutscenes, menus, photo mode hud.SetVisible(false) and true again; works from a UnityEvent or a Timeline Signal
Pause menu Tick Hide When Paused (hides while Time.timeScale is 0)
Split screen One HUD per camera, each in its camera's part of the screen. A waypoint is reached once, by the nearest player Give each HUD its Player; use Only these groups to show each player their own waypoints

Your UI #

Your setup What happens What you do
uGUI canvases, UI Toolkit menus The HUD is its own UI Toolkit panel. It never catches the mouse or gamepad focus: your buttons keep working through it Raise or lower Sorting Order to put it above or below your panels
Your own health bar, quest list or ammo panel The compass and radar move with the style (top or bottom, any corner, margins) Add Keep clear of my own HUD areas on the HUD: edge arrows stay out of them. Orange boxes show them in the Game view
Phones (notches, rounded corners) Everything stays inside the screen's safe area, re-read every frame Nothing
Your font Set it in the style's Text section
Your own HUD art Custom icons, backgrounds, arrows and radar player picture (Make it yours)
A completely different look (uGUI prefabs) The kit draws with UI Toolkit only Switch off the parts you replace and drive your own UI from TryGetView (Code and your own UI)

Your objects #

Your setup What happens What you do
Spawned enemies and pickups A prefab with a Waypoint shows up when it spawns and disappears when it's destroyed or switched off Nothing
Pooled objects A reused object keeps "reached" until you reset it Call ResetReached() when you take it from the pool
A spot with no object (a quest coordinate) Waypoint.CreateAt(position, "Drop Zone", WaypointIcon.Pin); move it with Position, delete with Remove()
Moving targets (NPCs, vehicles) The marker follows every frame, above the object's top For a steady point, turn Mark Above Object off and use Offset
Hundreds of waypoints Only the most important are drawn (objectives, priority, nearest): markers, arrows, compass icons and radar blips each have a limit in the style. Wall checks are spread over frames Nothing
Several waypoints with one name Labels aren't keys Use WaypointRegistry.FindById or FindAll

Your quests and saves #

Your setup What happens What you do
A quest system Waypoints only show where to go Call Show(), Hide(), SetAsCurrentObjective(), or use the On Reached event to tell your quest system
Visual Scripting, Timeline, UnityEvents Every action above is a public method or property Call them from a graph, a Signal or a UnityEvent
Saving WaypointSave.Capture() gives a short text for your save Store it; call WaypointSave.Restore(text) after loading. Waypoints in scenes loaded later pick up their state when they load
Prefabs placed many times Copies share a save id Scene check: New ids for all
Additive scenes, DontDestroyOnLoad HUD Waypoints join and leave as their scenes load and unload; the HUD keeps working Nothing
Domain Reload off Everything resets on Play Nothing

Not included #

A live camera minimap, fog of war, routes along paths, 3D beacons in the world, networking (each player's HUD is local) and VR. Arrows and distances are straight lines, not paths.
