All In One Surface Footstep Kit

Footsteps that sound like what your characters walk on. Add one component to any character and it plays the right sound, effect and footprint for wood, stone, grass, gravel, metal, water, carpet, snow or your own surfaces. When a step is silent, the debugger tells you why.

Read the documentation Join the Discord Features
  • Unity 6000.0+
  • Built-in, URP & HDRP
  • No dependencies
  • Full C# source

How it works

Footsteps on your character in three steps

  1. Pick your character

    The Setup Wizard works out the controller, where the feet are, 2D or 3D, and any footstep animation events. A prefab gets a variant; yours is never changed.

  2. Map your surfaces

    It lists every material, terrain layer and tile in your scene with a guess from its name. Check them and click Apply.

  3. Press Play

    Walk, run, crouch, jump and land. No sound? Why No Footstep? explains every step, with a one-click fix.

Features

Every surface, every controller, and an answer when something's off

Works with any character

No code
  • Steps from movement: Character Controller, Rigidbody, NavMesh Agent, Starter Assets, your own controller, NPCs and remote players.
  • Or exact timing from animation events (including Starter Assets' OnFootstep) or the foot bones.
  • Walk, run, sprint, crouch, jump and landings that get louder the harder you land. Moving platforms and stairs handled.

Finds the surface

  • Surface tags, terrain layers (blended), 2D tiles
  • Physics materials, materials, textures, names, Unity tags
  • Zones for water, puddles and rugs
  • A default, so no step is silent by accident

Why No Footstep?

  • Every step with its surface and the reason
  • Setup problems with one-click fixes
  • An in-game panel for builds
  • Scene Overview of every character and surface

Effects and footprints

  • Dust, splashes and snow puffs per surface
  • Footprints that fade, on the surfaces you choose
  • Pooled: no garbage, nothing pink in any pipeline

Make it yours

  • Realistic, Arcade, Stealth and Heavy presets
  • Per-character volume, pitch, stride, gear sounds
  • Your own audio system (FMOD, Wwise) through one interface

AI hearing

  • Guards hear steps: louder on metal, quiet crouched on grass
  • Walls muffle; a UnityEvent with the position

Sounds included

CC0 and our own
  • Eight surfaces ready to use: wood, stone, grass, gravel, metal, water, carpet and snow, plus an armour jingle for gear sounds.
  • Three demos: a walk through a house, garden and guarded yard; a gallery of every surface; and a 2D platformer.

Good to know

Limits, stated up front

  • No foot IK: the kit reads foot bones, it doesn't move them.
  • FMOD and Wwise work through one small interface you implement; no ready-made integration is included.
