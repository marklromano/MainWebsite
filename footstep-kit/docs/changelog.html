Changelog

All notable changes to the All In One Surface Footstep Kit. The format follows Keep a Changelog; versions follow Semantic Versioning.

[1.0.1] - 2026-11-08 #

Smoother fitting into your own game. Nothing to do when you update: your settings and characters keep working.

Added #

  • Pause Auto Steps / Resume Auto Steps (and SetAutoStepsPaused): no automatic steps while swimming, climbing a ladder, sliding or in a cutscene; your own Step() calls still play, and resuming never plays a stray landing.
  • ResetMotion(): call it after a teleport, respawn or when a pooled character is reused, so the move is never a step.
  • Surface queries without a character: library.TryResolveAt(position, out result) and library.ResolveHit(hit), the same answer a footstep gets (zones included), for bullet impacts, dropped items or tyres.
  • Min Event Weight (Animation Events): in blend trees and crossfades where no clip has half the weight, the strongest event in the frame now plays, so steps are never lost and never doubled.
  • Setup check: warns when another script on the character plays its own footsteps (double steps), with a Select button.
  • Documentation: a new "Your controller and game" page (custom controllers, root motion, swimming and ladders, teleports, multiplayer, split screen, pause, many NPCs, custom terrain shaders, runtime levels).

Fixed #

  • Surface Footsteps on a child model now ignores every collider of the character above it (a Character Controller, or a Rigidbody with a capsule), not only the model's own.
  • Big terrains no longer hitch or allocate a large temporary array on the first step: layers are read in small bands when the scene starts (library.PrewarmTerrains() after loading a terrain at runtime).

[1.0.0] - 2026-11-07 #

First release.

Added #

  • Surface Footsteps: footsteps for any character (player, NPC, remote player, 2D), from movement, animation events or foot bones, with jumps, landings, crouching, running and sprinting.
  • Surfaces and the Surface Library: sounds, effects, footprints and AI loudness per surface, found from Surface Tags, terrain layers, tiles, physics materials, materials, textures, names and Unity tags, with a default surface.
  • Footstep Zones for water, puddles and rugs (replace the ground, or add on top).
  • Footstep Profiles with four presets (Realistic, Arcade, Stealth, Heavy) and per-character overrides.
  • Why No Footstep? debugger window and in-game panel (with a Text Size setting), Scene Overview, Setup Wizard, Help and Support.
  • Footstep Listener for AI hearing.
  • Make Surface From Selected Clips: turn your own sounds into a surface in one click.
  • 13 sample surfaces with more than 200 sounds (credits in Third-Party Notices.txt).
  • Demos: Showcase, Feature Gallery, 2D side view.
