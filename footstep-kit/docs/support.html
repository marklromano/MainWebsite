Support

Need help? Every message gets an answer.

  • Documentation: https://raregeometry.github.io/footstep-kit/docs/
  • Discord: https://discord.gg/AmpBDxRAKu (ask in #help)
  • Report a bug: https://github.com/raregeometry/Support/issues
  • Website: https://raregeometry.github.io/footstep-kit/
  • Email: raregeometrysupport@gmail.com

In Unity: Tools > All In One Surface Footstep Kit > Help and Support, then Copy Support Info (kit version, Unity version, render pipeline and platform) and paste it in your message. A screenshot of the Why No Footstep? window helps too.

Enjoying the kit? A short review on the Asset Store helps a lot.
