Troubleshooting

Start with Tools > All In One Surface Footstep Kit > Why No Footstep?: it checks the setup and explains every step.

What you see Why, and what to do
No steps at all Is there an Audio Listener in the scene? Is the character moving faster than Min Speed (0.25 m/s)? The window's Live view shows both.
"No ground" in the debugger The ground check misses the ground: the feet are in the wrong place (Feet Offset), the check is too short (Check Depth), or the ground's layer isn't in Ground Layers. The setup check finds which and fixes it.
The wrong surface plays The debugger says why each surface was chosen. Map the material in Scene Overview > Surfaces, or put a Footstep Surface Tag on the object.
Everything sounds like the default Nothing matched. Map your materials (Setup Wizard step 3), or add keywords to your surfaces that appear in your material names.
Double steps Another script plays footsteps too (your controller, Starter Assets): turn its footsteps off. The setup check names it, and the wizard can silence Starter Assets. With Animation Events, check the events aren't on two clips that play at once at full weight.
Steps while swimming, climbing or sliding Call Pause Auto Steps when it starts and Resume Auto Steps when it ends. See Your controller and game.
A step or landing after a teleport or respawn Call ResetMotion() right after moving the character.
Wrong sound on a custom terrain shader (vertex colours, MicroSplat) The kit reads Unity terrain layers only. Use Footstep Zones or Surface Tags for those areas.
Steps too fast or too slow Change the strides in the profile, or Stride Scale on the character.
Steps while standing on a moving platform Make sure the platform has a collider under the feet and isn't marked Static.
No footprints The surface needs Footprints ticked and the profile needs Show Footprints and a Footprint Picture. They don't show in 2D side view.
Steps can't be heard far away Raise Max Distance in the profile, or set Spatial to Flat for the player.
Pink effects or footprints Give the material the kit's "All In One Surface Footstep Kit/Unlit Transparent" shader (it supports Built-in, URP and HDRP).
Terrain painted at runtime plays old layers Call library.RefreshTerrain(terrainData).
