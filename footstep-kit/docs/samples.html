Docs/Footstep Kit/Start here

Demo scenes

Open them from Tools > All In One Surface Footstep Kit > Open Demo. Controls: WASD or the left stick to move, Shift to run (hold longer to sprint), C or Ctrl to crouch, Space to jump. Click the game to look around with the mouse; Escape frees the mouse. M shows the Make it yours panel, P changes the feel, F turns footprints on and off, F4 shows the Why No Footstep? panel.

Showcase #

A walk from a wooden house (with a carpet) out to a stone patio and a garden: a terrain painted with grass, a gravel path, a snowy corner, a mud patch and fallen leaves. Cross the pond on the wooden bridge or wade through it, climb the metal stairs, then sneak past the guard in the gravel yard: crouch and it can't hear you, sprint and it turns to look. A knight NPC patrols with heavy steps and plate armour, and a robot patrols on its own metal feet whatever the ground (a Forced Surface); both are moved by a plain script that knows nothing about footsteps.

Feature Gallery #

One pad of each of the 13 surfaces, a ledge to jump off for landings, a moving platform (standing still on it plays no steps), and a puddle that adds splashes on top of the stone. The Make it yours panel switches presets, footprints, effects, 3D or flat sound and volume while you play.

2D Side View #

A platformer level made of tiles: grass, wood, stone, metal, sand, mud, snow, ice, carpet and gravel. Each tile plays its own surface; the pool at the end splashes. The character uses Surface Footsteps set to 2D Side View.

All demo content is generated, uses only what ships, and works in Built-in, URP and HDRP.
