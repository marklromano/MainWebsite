Quick start

Footsteps on your own character in about five minutes. No code.

1. Open the Setup Wizard #

Tools > All In One Surface Footstep Kit > Setup Wizard. You can also right-click a character in the Hierarchy and choose All In One Surface Footstep Kit > Add Footsteps (Setup Wizard), or right-click a prefab in the Project window.

2. Pick your character #

Drag it into Character, or select it and click Use selected. The wizard looks at it and tells you what it found:

  • the controller (Character Controller, Rigidbody, Rigidbody 2D, NavMesh Agent or none) and where the feet are,
  • whether it's the player (tagged Player or has a camera): the player's own steps play flat, the same in both ears,
  • whether its animations already have footstep events, and its foot bones on a humanoid,
  • whether another script already plays footsteps (Unity's Starter Assets do): the wizard can silence it.

A prefab is never changed: the wizard saves a Prefab Variant beside it called "Name (Footsteps)".

3. Choose how it feels #

Pick a Preset (Realistic, Arcade, Stealth or Heavy) and where steps come from. Automatic works with every controller: a step every stride while the character moves on the ground.

4. Map your surfaces #

The wizard lists every material, terrain layer and tile in the open scene and what it plays now. It guesses from names ("Grass_02" is Grass, "WoodenBridge" is Wood). Change any guess, then click Apply. Using the sample library is fine to start; click Make my own copy before you change its sounds, so kit updates never overwrite your changes.

5. Add Footsteps and play #

Click Add Footsteps, then press Play and walk. The wizard checks the setup and lists anything that would stop steps from playing, each with a fix button.

No sound, or the wrong one? Open Tools > All In One Surface Footstep Kit > Why No Footstep?. It shows every step with its surface and why it was chosen, and the reason for any step it skipped.
