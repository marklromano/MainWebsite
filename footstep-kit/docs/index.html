All In One Surface Footstep Kit documentation

Footsteps that sound like what your characters walk on, for any controller in 3D and 2D, with effects, footprints, AI hearing and a debugger that says why a step was silent.

  • Unity 6000.0+
  • Built-in, URP & HDRP
  • No dependencies
  • Full C# source
  • Version 0.1.0

Coming soon to the Asset Store.

Get started

Quick start: five minutes

  1. Import the package. Everything lands in Assets/AllInOneFootstepKit/. Try Tools > All In One Surface Footstep Kit > Open Demo > Showcase and press Play.
  2. Open Tools > All In One Surface Footstep Kit > Setup Wizard and pick your character.
  3. Check the surfaces it found in your scene, click Apply, then Add Footsteps.
  4. Press Play and walk. No sound? Why No Footstep? tells you why, with a fix button.
