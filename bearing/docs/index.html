Bearing documentation

Compass, minimap, world markers and a full-screen map built from your level, with routes that follow your roads, fast travel, fog of war and floors for buildings.

  • Unity 6000.0+
  • Built-in, URP & HDRP
  • Both input systems
  • Full C# source
  • Version 1.0.0

Coming soon to the Asset Store.

Get started

Quick start: five minutes

  1. Import the package. Bearing lands in Assets/RareGeometry/Bearing/.
  2. Open Tools > Bearing > Setup Wizard. It checks your scene and adds the HUD, your player and camera.
  3. Add a Waypoint to a landmark and pick an icon, or add places from the Chart Table.
  4. Bake the map from the Waypoint Map component, press Play and press M for the full map.
