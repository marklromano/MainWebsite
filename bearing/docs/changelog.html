Changelog

All notable changes to Bearing. Format: Keep a Changelog. Versions: Semantic Versioning.

[1.0.0] - Unreleased #

Changed #

  • Help and Support shows the website (https://raregeometry.github.io/bearing/) and the bug tracker; the online manual link appears once the manual is on the website. Shared Core synced to 8d41ac2 (studio website, bug tracker and support email live).
  • Shared Core synced to 5f55d5d (test-only: registry tests no longer depend on test order).
  • Clean map floor pictures (a height band) are drawn flat, without relief shading, so floors read cleanly round furniture and walls.
  • Calm defaults: markers on screen within 40 m, edge arrows for the objective, the compass within 150 m; caps 8 markers, 4 edge arrows, 10 compass icons. Existing styles get the new rules too; pick Everything in Showing the right icons for the old behaviour.
  • Waypoint Route: Source is now Mode (NavMesh is Fastest, Manual is My Points); saved scenes keep their setting.
  • Default layout: the objective tracker's top lines up with the compass bar's (top right, one 28 px margin with the minimap). Existing style assets keep their saved positions; set Style > Tracker > Edge Gap to 28 and Radar > Margin to 28 for the new layout.

Added #

  • The minimap never ends in a hard line or shows the world past the map: its frame is solid wherever a map is shown, and past the map picture it carries on with the map's own edge (the sea round an island), smoothed, out past the frame's diagonal so a turning minimap is covered at every angle. Edge Mode on the Waypoint Map sets it for the minimap and the full map: Continue Edge Colour (default), Stop At Edge (the minimap's view stays inside the map and the arrow moves towards the edge instead) or Themed Surround (the look's frame colour). Range rings and the centre cross step back over a map picture.
  • Fast travel lands with the place in full view: clear of it (by its size, so a tent, a cave or a tall stone fits the view), on open level ground, never inside anything, facing it with the camera tipped to its middle. Waypoint Travel Point has Arrival Gap and Arrival Max Distance and raises AnyArrived with the view pitch for your camera; TravelLanding.Find works out the spot for your own travel code; Make It A Travel Point places its Arrival the same way.
  • The full-screen map, reworked: the map runs on to the frame's edge with no hard cut (its own edge, smoothed, carried out to the frame), Edge Mode on the Waypoint Map (Continue Edge Colour, Stop At Edge, Themed Surround) for the minimap and the full map alike, an Edge Vignette, one side panel with the map key and a count per kind (terrain folded away), names that never overlap (more important names win; others move above their icon or wait for a closer zoom), a round "+n" badge on merged pins, and a back button in the header for a building's map.
  • The place card and pin editor: a solid card that stays inside the map frame and flips near its edges; the pin's icon, an editable name (a dim hint until typed) and its distance in the header; icon and colour choices with clear selected states; equal-width buttons with Track (or Fast travel) first and Delete asking once more. A click on a place selects it (fast travel is a card button, with Open World asking first); a right-click (or a tap, or the gamepad) marks the map.
  • Show Beacons on the HUD: one switch that hides every world beacon, even places set to always show one (a player's choice wins).
  • The mouse cursor while playing: Bearing frees and shows it whenever its map or player options are up, players can hold Alt for a free cursor at any time, a short hint says so, and in browser builds a lost or refused pointer lock no longer leaves the cursor hidden (Cursor Policy, Free Cursor Key, Show Cursor When Free, Cursor Hint on the HUD; WaypointHud.WantsCursor for your controller; BearingInput.KeyHeld).
  • Fixed: a search area under a minimap blip could spill past the minimap's frame (a soft grey blob at the rim, bright at large minimap sizes). It now shows only in range and never wider than the room to the rim.
  • Teammates' shared marks and pings are drawn filled in the sender's colour (Waypoint.FillMarker, settable for any place from code).
  • Compass skins (Bar, Open Rail, Fantasy, Sci-fi) with an optional gentle curve, and minimap frames (Plain, Fantasy, Sci-fi) whose north follows true north; drawn as vector art in each look's colours, with less detail on small screens.
  • Polygon regions: draw a region's outline in the Scene view (snapped to the ground) or fill it from the map picture (flood fill with a tolerance); discovery, fog, reveals and place counts follow the shape; faint region outlines on the full-screen map and the minimap; the Chart Table's Map tab lists regions with Fill From Map.
  • Showing the right icons: a distance rule per display (screen, edge, compass, minimap, map) with presets (Objective only, Nearby only, Discovered places, Everything), per category and per place; the objective always shows; players can choose (Icons); overlapping map pins merge into one with "+n"; the Chart Table shows where each place appears (Shows on) and applies presets.
  • Touch on the full-screen map: drag, pinch to zoom, tap a place for its card. Route line kinds (dashes, dots, arrows, solid) and the walked part kept as a grey line on the minimap and map.
  • A coordinate grid on the full-screen map (A1, B2...), off by default; a heartbeat that quickens near the objective (Waypoint Sounds > Heartbeat).
  • Animation choices: Motion presets (Off, Subtle, Standard, Lively, Arcade) set every animation at once; new reveal kinds Drop, Spin and Wiggle; an Appeared animation for places added while the game runs; a Ping animation; the objective's pulse as a ring, breathing, or both, with its speed; the banner's entrance (slide down, slide up, zoom, fade); an optional flowing route line. Reduced motion turns all of them off.
  • Route modes, like a game's GPS: Fastest (NavMesh), Prefer roads and trails, Roads and trails only, Straight line, My points and Off; a Fallback when there is no path (Straight line, Compass only, Hide); Show When (Always, Tracked or set places only, Map open only, A few seconds after setting). The route stays steady while followed and is worked out again when the player strays. Waypoint Road marks roads and trails (joined where they meet). Each place can ask for its own mode; players can pick theirs in the options panel (Player Options > Route options).
  • The route on screen is calm: soft dashes laid on the ground, fading in a few steps ahead and out at Draw Ahead, narrower with distance, hidden behind walls and hills, never over the HUD.
  • Region reveals: lookout towers, map items and story events that clear a circle or a whole region over time and discover the places inside as the clearing reaches them; "Revealed by" on a region; saved.
  • Region progress on the full-screen map ("Old Mill 3/7").
  • Map search: type a place's name or category; the map zooms, turns to its floor, centres on it and opens its card.
  • Beacons: a pillar of light over the objective (or the tracked place, or every place the player knows), drawn by the HUD in every render pipeline; at most the nearest 6 at once by default (Max Beacons, 0 = no limit).
  • One text table for every word (Collect Words fills it), an optional Unity Localization bridge, and your own translator.
  • Travel points that do the whole job: Arrival spot, unlock rule, locked reason, cost check and the move itself.
  • Chart Table: "As the player" (try a place in Play Mode) and "What shows at each zoom".
  • Tools > Bearing > Create Example Map; GameObject > Bearing > Split Screen; Waypoint Save Keys (F5 and F9); ShowBanner for your own banners.
  • Style: Layout (nudge, size and fade the compass, minimap, tracker and banner), tracker display, banner fade and slide, map opening animation, region and panel colours; places can keep their own icon colours.
  • The Clean map skips meshes it cannot read instead of stopping; setup only picks a camera that is clearly the player's.
  • First release. Waypoint component, on-screen markers, edge arrows with distance, compass bar, minimap (radar) and full-screen map, a Clean map maker (plus photo from above or your own picture), fog of war and region discovery, four looks (Explorer, Tactical, Minimal, Holo), Setup Wizard, Why Can't I See It? debugger with scene check, saving, demos and documentation.
  • Players can place their own marker on the full-screen map (mouse or gamepad); it shows everywhere and is saved.
  • Gamepad control of the full-screen map with the Input System package (move, zoom, centre, place, close).
  • Show on: Full-screen map switch, and one-click presets (Everywhere, Radar and map, Map only, Screen only).
  • Every waypoint and map region carries a shared Stable Id, so saves and other products find the same object across sessions; duplicated objects get a new id automatically.
  • Saving through the shared save contract: SaveService.Save("slot") and SaveService.Load("slot") store Bearing's state in a JSON file per slot, or in a save product when one is installed. WaypointSave.Capture and Restore still work with your own save system.
  • Diagnostics window (Tools > Bearing > Diagnostics) with the scene check and the shared foundation's checks.
  • Place states on every waypoint: Undiscovered, Discovered, Active (the objective), Completed and Hidden, each with its own look on the marker, edge arrow, compass, minimap, full-screen map and legend; one change updates every display in the same frame, with C# events and UnityEvents.
  • Chart Table (Tools > Bearing > Chart Table): every place in one list with search and filters, inline editing, make places from the selection, an objective-state test panel, objective chains, state bindings and a map calibration wizard.
  • Discovery: when near, when seen, with a region, or from code; a discovery banner with a queue and display options.
  • Fast travel: discovered places can be travel points picked on the full-screen map; your game does the move.
  • Chart Table demo scene.
  • Reveal animations when a place changes state (flip the "?" to the icon, pop, pulse, ring), per change, per place and per display, with easing, scale, sound and an event.
  • The full-screen map explains itself: a controls bar with keys and gamepad buttons, a card for the place under the pointer with a Fast travel button, a first-open hint, and L to hide the legends.
  • Clean maps draw bridges over water and paths (new Bridge category).
  • World scan: a pulse that discovers and highlights places in range (radius, speed, cooldown, key, line of sight, categories, sound, events), drawn on screen, minimap and map.
  • Moving entities: enemies, allies, vehicles and other players with faction colours, icons that turn to their heading, show rules (always, in range, line of sight, when spotted), a fading last known position, smoothing, trails, and per-player visibility in split screen.
  • Marker sharing: players share pins, stamps and pings through any networking (a transport interface; a local transport for split screen; a Netcode for GameObjects transport when that package is installed; share codes without networking), each player's marks in their colour, gated by the player map permissions.
  • Map areas: a building, cave or section has its own map that shows while the player is inside (priority, height range, fade, outline on the map around it, step out and back in). Floors: Bake All Floors (roof and upper floors cut away), floor tracking with hysteresis, objective floor shown, unvisited floors can be locked, other-floor places dimmed or hidden, fog per floor. Save format 5. Chart Table area and floor columns; diagnostics.
  • Players on the map: pins and stamps with icon, colour, name and note; Track any place; fast travel with its status, reasons, a confirm and your own check; all gated by a Player Map Permissions asset (Guided Story, Open World, Survival Cartographer). Reduced Motion. Save format 4.
  • Chart Table: the Places list fits narrow windows (columns tuck away in order, row tooltips), category dropdowns, per-place icon colour; window tab icons match Unity's own size.
  • Routes: a Waypoint Route leads to the objective or a target along the NavMesh walking path, your own points or a straight line, drawn on screen along the ground, on the full-screen map, on the minimap and as a cue on the compass bar; with no path it falls back to a straight line and Diagnostics says why.
  • Objective tracker: the current objectives and up to three tracked places with distances.
  • Compass bearing readout (NE 45° or 045°) and height arrows on markers and edge arrows.
  • Map floors (a picture per floor, floor buttons on the full-screen map, the minimap follows the player's floor).
  • Map legend with clickable filters by state and category.
  • Saving keeps tracked places, map filters and the route target.
