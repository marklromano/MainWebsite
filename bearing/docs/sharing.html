Sharing markers with other players

Players can share their pins and stamps, and ping a spot ("look here"), with the other players: co-op, split screen or online. Bearing does not tie you to a networking library.

  1. Add Waypoint Marker Sharing to an object in the scene (one per local player). Set the player's name and colour.
  2. Give it a transport: - Split screen / local co-op: sharing.Transport = new LocalMarkerTransport("my game"); for each player (same channel name). - Netcode for GameObjects: add Netcode Marker Transport to the same object. It appears only when Netcode for GameObjects (2.0 or newer) is installed. The host passes each player's markers on to everyone. - Anything else (Mirror, Fish-Net, Photon, Steam, your own): implement IMarkerShareTransport (an id, Send, Received): send the bytes, raise Received with the bytes that arrive.
  3. That's it: new pins and stamps are shared as they are placed, moved and removed (Share Automatically), and the other players' marks appear in their colour with their name. A ping shows for Ping Seconds with a ring.

What players may do comes from the HUD's Player Map Permissions (Sharing): share their marks, see the others', remove the others' from their own map, ping, and the most shared marks shown at once.

Without networking: sharing.CodeFor(mark) gives a short code (BRG1. and letters) to paste into chat or a forum; sharing.ImportCode(code) shows it.

From code: sharing.Share(mark), sharing.Ping(position), sharing.RemoveShared(place), events Shared and Received. A marker travels as SharedMarker (about 40 bytes plus its words): ToBytes, FromBytes.
