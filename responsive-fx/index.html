Responsive FX

Game feel for every hit, step and impact. A sword on armour sparks, rings and shakes the camera; a bullet through glass leaves shards and a crack; boots crunch on gravel and go quiet on grass, and the guard down the hall hears them. One system runs it all: a contact lands on a surface, and the surface answers with a sound, an effect, a mark, screen feedback and a noise your AI can hear.

How it works

A contact lands. The surface answers.

  1. Tag your surfaces

    17 ready surfaces (wood, stone, metal, glass, snow, water and more). Materials, tiles and terrain layers are matched for you.

  2. Add it to your characters

    The Setup Wizards add hit feedback and footsteps to any character, with any controller. Your prefabs get variants; originals stay as they are.

  3. Tune it in Impact Lab

    Every surface against every kind of contact in one grid. Click a square, press Listen, adjust. The Trace says what played and why.

Features

Everything that touches anything

Hit feedback

The headline
  • The whole hit: damage numbers, flash, hitstop, shake, knockback, squash, health and boss bars, sparks and the kill moment.
  • Four looks restyle numbers, bars, flash and sparks together; genre presets, a Juice slider and the Recipe Studio timeline (Impact first, Heavy blow, Final blow).

Footsteps

  • 2D and 3D, any controller, any animation or none
  • Footprints that stay; effects per surface
  • Weather and footwear change the answer

Surface impacts

  • Bullets in one line; marks that stay on what they hit
  • Physics objects answer their own bumps, slides and rolls
  • Breakables crack, cloud and shatter

AI hearing

  • Listeners hear steps and impacts by loudness and distance
  • Sound passes through open doors, not walls
  • Trails AI can follow

The player's side

  • The Sound Ring: where sounds came from
  • Damage direction, low-health cue, captions, a noise meter

Impact Lab

One window
  • Every surface against every contact, colour-coded parts
  • The Event Trace and the Response Graph for special cases

Why no feedback?

  • Every part of every hit and step: played or skipped, and why
  • An in-game panel for footsteps (F4)

Players get their own options

You decide
  • A ready options screen: shake, flashes, numbers, volumes, captions, vibration and presets such as Reduced motion.
  • Gated by you: you choose what players may change. Options never change damage, timing or what AI hears.

Good to know

Limits, stated up front

  • Responsive FX shows and sounds contacts; it doesn't decide them. No weapons, AI behaviour or stats (a basic Health component is included).
  • Feedback is local: play it on each client from your own networked event (a Netcode bridge is included).
