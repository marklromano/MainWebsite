Make it yours: profiles and presets

Everything a player hears, sees or that an AI reacts to can be changed or switched off, without code.

Presets first #

A Footstep Profile sets how steps feel. Start from a preset, then change any value:

Preset Feel
Realistic Natural strides, gentle variation, subtle dust and prints.
Arcade Snappy and punchy: louder, bigger puffs, longer-lasting prints.
Stealth Quiet: very soft crouching, and noise matters more to AI.
Heavy Big, slow characters: long strides, low pitch, heavy landings.

Create one with Create > Responsive FX > Footstep Profile or the New profile from preset button on Surface Footsteps. The profile inspector has a Preset picker and Apply preset at the top (one undo step). Without a profile asset, Surface Footsteps uses its Preset directly.

Everything in a profile #

Group Settings
Volume and pitch Volume, pitch, pitch variation, walk / run / sprint / crouch volume.
Stride Walk, run, sprint and crouch stride; run and sprint speeds; minimum speed; min step interval.
Jump and landing Play jump, play land, jump speed, min air time, soft and hard landing speeds, landing volume.
Effects Show effects, effect scale.
Footprints Show footprints, footprint picture, material, size, lifetime, fade, maximum at once.
3D sound Spatial blend, min and max distance, rolloff, Audio Mixer group, priority.
Noise for AI Emit noise; how far walk, run, sprint, crouch and landing steps carry.

Per character #

Each Surface Footsteps can override its profile: volume, pitch and stride scales, footprints / effects / noise on or off, flat or 3D sound, a forced surface, surface swaps and gear sounds. See Surface Footsteps.

Your own sounds, pictures and audio system #

  • Every sound is a clip you choose, per surface and movement.
  • Every effect is your own prefab; footprints use your own picture (a right foot pointing up; the left is mirrored).
  • Bring your own audio: put a component implementing IFootstepSoundPlayer in Sound Player and Responsive FX hands it every step (FMOD, Wwise, your manager). See Extending.
  • Switch anything off: no effects, no footprints, no AI noise, no jump sound, or mute a surface.

The demos' Make it yours panel (M) switches presets, footprints, effects, flat or 3D sound and volume while you play.
