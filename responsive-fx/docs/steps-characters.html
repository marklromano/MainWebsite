Docs/Responsive FX/Footsteps

Surface Footsteps (your characters)

Surface Footsteps goes on each character that should make steps: the player, NPCs, animals, remote players in a multiplayer game. It doesn't move the character and doesn't need to know how it moves.

The main choices (top of the inspector) #

Setting What it does
Library Your surfaces.
Profile How steps feel. Empty: the Preset below. New profile from preset makes an asset you can change and share.
Steps come from Automatic, Animation Events, Foot Bones or Manual. See When steps happen.
Dimension 3D, 2D Side View or 2D Top-Down. See 2D games.

A status line under the title says "Ready" or the first problem, with Why No Footstep? and Setup Wizard buttons. In Play Mode it shows the last step and Test a step / Test a landing buttons play one on demand.

Ground check and feet #

Setting What it does
Ground Layers Layers that count as ground. Leave on Everything unless the check hits things it shouldn't.
Feet Offset Where the feet are, relative to the pivot. 0 when the pivot is at the feet.
Check Height, Check Depth The ground check starts this far above the feet and reaches this far below. Raise the depth for stairs.
Foot Spacing Steps and footprints alternate left and right of the centre.
Left Foot, Right Foot Bones for the Foot Bones trigger and exact footprints. The wizard finds them on humanoids.
Crouch Detection Collider Height (crouched when the capsule shrinks to 75% or less) or Manual (SetCrouching).
Floor Tilemaps Top-down 2D only: the tilemaps that make the floor.

The character's own colliders are always ignored by the ground check.

This character (overrides the profile) #

Volume, pitch and stride scales; Footprints, Effects and Noise each Use Profile, On or Off; Spatial (Use Profile, Flat or Full 3D); Forced Surface (a robot always sounds like Metal); Surface Swaps (this giant's Wood sounds different); Gear Sounds played with every step (armour, chains, a squeaky shoe).

Sound player and events #

  • Sound Player: a component that implements IFootstepSoundPlayer plays the sounds instead (FMOD, Wwise, your own audio manager). See Extending.
  • On Step, On Jump, On Land: UnityEvents for no-code wiring (camera bob, controller rumble).

Swimming, ladders and teleports #

  • Pause Auto Steps / Resume Auto Steps (also callable from UnityEvents): no automatic steps, jumps or landings while swimming, climbing or in a cutscene. Your own Step() calls still play.
  • ResetMotion(): call it after a teleport, respawn or reusing a pooled character.

See Your controller and game for more.

Moving platforms, stairs, teleports #

Standing still on a moving platform plays nothing: Responsive FX takes the platform's movement out. Stairs work with the default check depth; raise Check Depth for tall steps. A jump of more than 4 m in one frame is never a step.
