Profiles, presets and Juice

A Hit Feedback Profile holds everything a hit plays. Share one between all your goblins, another for bosses.

Make one #

  • Assets > Create > Responsive FX > Profile from Preset > (preset): a new profile already set to that feel.
  • Or press Save As Profile on a Hit Feedback that uses a preset (saved in Assets/RareGeometryData/ResponsiveFX/Profiles).

The presets #

Preset Feel
Arcade Big, bright and bouncy, with strong shake and hitstop. Kill pop and a screen flash.
Action RPG Clear numbers that add up, crits that pop, a bar on hit.
Souls-like Heavy and grounded: a global hitstop, rim flash, slow drain, quiet numbers at the hit point.
Cartoon Squash and stretch, bouncy numbers ending in "!", bright colours, sparks and puffs.
Shooter Small fast numbers at the hit point, a rim flash, light shake, no freeze.
Idle and Mobile Many numbers that add up, short forms (1.2K), bigger numbers for bigger amounts, no shake.
Minimal A clean flash and a small number, nothing else.
Existing Game (safe) For a game that already has combat: numbers, flash, bar, sound and sparks only. Never moves, freezes or scales your objects, never touches your camera or time.

The buttons at the top of the profile inspector apply a preset. Your clips, icons, font and particle prefab are kept.

Juice #

One slider for everything. 0 keeps only numbers, bars and sounds; 1 is the preset; 2 doubles flash, shake, hitstop, knockback, squash and particles. There is also a master Juice for the whole game in HitFeedbackSettings.Juice, for an options menu.

Scale with amount #

Turn on Scale with amount and bigger hits feel bigger: shake, hitstop, knockback and squash scale with the amount compared with Full strength amount. (Number size by amount is a separate setting under Numbers.)

Kinds #

Each kind (Normal, Critical, Heal, Shield, Miss, Block, Damage Over Time, Status) has a colour, a colour-blind safe colour, a word, a suffix, a size, a strength, an icon and its sounds.

The sections #

Numbers, Flash, Hitstop, Camera shake and punch, Knockback, Squash, Floating health bar, Sound, Particles and Kill moment. Each is on its own page here. The inspector shows a live preview of the numbers as you change them.
