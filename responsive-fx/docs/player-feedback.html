Player feedback

Add Player Hit Feedback to your player (next to its Hit Feedback or Health). It reacts to the player's hits by itself; or call PlayerHit(hitInfo) from your own code.

Part What it does Settings
Damage vignette The screen edges flash a colour; bigger hits come closer to the full strength. Colour, strength, time
Damage direction An arrow around the screen centre points at where the hit came from (its Source, Direction or Point). Colour, time, radius, size
Low health The screen edges pulse while health is below a threshold (a steady glow with Reduce Motion). Threshold, colour, rate, strength
Rumble The gamepad rumbles: stronger on criticals, softer on blocks and damage over time. Needs the Input System package; without it, it does nothing. Low and high motor strength, time

Heals, misses and status words play nothing here. Turn rumble off for the whole game with HitFeedbackSettings.Rumble. Use your own rumble (another input asset) by setting PlayerHitFeedback.RumbleHandler.
