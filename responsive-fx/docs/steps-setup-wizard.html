Docs/Responsive FX/Footsteps

Setup Wizard

Tools > Responsive FX > Setup Wizard sets up footsteps on your own character and maps the surfaces in your scene. Nothing changes until you click Add Footsteps or Apply, and each is one undo step.

1. Your character #

What it checks What it does with it
Character Controller or Capsule Collider Puts the feet at the bottom of the capsule (the pivot is often in the middle).
Rigidbody 2D Picks 2D side view when it has gravity, 2D top-down when it doesn't.
No controller Steps follow the object's movement, whatever moves it. Feet go to the bottom of the model.
Tagged Player, or has a camera Turns on Flat sound: the player's own steps sound the same in both ears.
Humanoid Animator Finds the foot bones, so footprints land under each foot.
Footstep animation events Recommends Animation Events for exact timing.
Another script with footstep clips Offers to silence it (sets its footstep volume to 0), so you don't hear both.

Prefabs: the wizard saves a Prefab Variant called "Name (Footsteps)" next to your prefab. Use the variant in your scenes. Your prefab stays exactly as it was and still receives your changes.

2. How it feels #

  • Preset: Realistic, Arcade, Stealth or Heavy. See Make it yours.
  • Steps come from: Automatic, Animation Events, Foot Bones or Manual. See When steps happen.
  • Flat sound (the player): on for the character you control, off for NPCs.

3. Your surfaces #

Pick a Surface Library. The sample one has 13 surfaces with sounds: Wood, Stone, Grass, Gravel, Dirt, Mud, Sand, Leaves, Metal, Water, Carpet, Snow and Ice. Make my own copy copies it and its surfaces into Assets/RareGeometryData/ResponsiveFX, where updates never touch them.

The table lists each material, terrain layer and tile in the open scene, how often it's used, and what it plays now (and why). The wizard's guess is already picked; change any, then click Apply to save them in the library as rules. Show selects an object that uses it.

Done #

After Add Footsteps the wizard runs the setup check and lists any problem with a fix button, for example "no Audio Listener in the scene" or "the ground is on a layer Ground Layers leaves out".
