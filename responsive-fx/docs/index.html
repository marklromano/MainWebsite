Docs/Responsive FX

Responsive FX documentation

Game feel for every hit, step and impact: sound, effects, marks, screen feedback and noise your AI hears, tuned in one window and adjustable by your players. The Feedback Player gives every other moment (a pickup, a jump, a button) its own list of feedbacks.

  • Unity 6000.0+
  • Built-in, URP & HDRP
  • No dependencies
  • Full C# source
  • Version 1.0.0

Coming soon to the Asset Store.

Get started

Quick start: five minutes

  1. Import the package. Responsive FX lands in Assets/RareGeometry/ResponsiveFX/.
  2. Open Tools > Responsive FX > Demos > Response Playground and press Play. Walk, strike (click), shoot (F) and open the player options (O).
  3. Run the Setup Wizards (hits and steps) on your own character. Your prefab gets a variant; the original stays as it is.
  4. Open Tools > Responsive FX > Impact Lab, click a square, press Listen and tune it.
