Support

Stuck, or found a bug in the Responsive FX? Every message is read by the developer who wrote it.

Where to ask #

  • Discord (questions, ideas, show and tell): https://discord.gg/AmpBDxRAKu
  • Bug tracker (report a bug and follow it until it's fixed): https://github.com/raregeometry/Support
  • Email: raregeometrysupport@gmail.com
  • Website and documentation: https://raregeometry.github.io

Reporting a bug #

Send these and it can be fixed fast:

  1. The support info. In Tools > Responsive FX > Help and Support, click Copy system info: it puts the kit's version, Unity's version and your render pipeline on the clipboard. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  2. What you did, step by step, and what you expected to happen.
  3. What happened instead, with a screenshot if it shows.
  4. Any red errors from the Console (click one, then copy its full text).
