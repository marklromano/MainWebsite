Footsteps

Footsteps that sound like what your characters walk on. Add one component to any character and it plays the right sound, effect and footprint for wood, stone, grass, gravel, dirt, mud, sand, leaves, metal, water, carpet, snow, ice or your own surfaces. It works with any character controller, in 3D and 2D, and when a step is silent the Why No Footstep? debugger tells you why in plain words, with a one-click fix.

What it does #

Part What it gives you
Surface Footsteps Footsteps on any character: player, NPC, remote player or 2D hero. Walk, run, sprint, crouch, jump and land.
Character types Two legs, four legs, hopping, many legs, wheels, slithering and hovering: each steps, sounds and leaves marks its own way.
Surfaces and the Surface Library Each surface's sounds, effect, footprints and AI loudness, and how Responsive FX recognises it in your scene.
Surface Zones Water, puddles and rugs: replace the ground's sound, or add a splash on top.
Profiles and presets Realistic, Arcade, Stealth and Heavy feels in one click, then change any value.
Effects and footprints Pooled dust, splashes and fading footprints that work in Built-in, URP and HDRP.
AI hearing A Footstep Listener lets guards hear steps: louder on metal, quieter crouched on grass.
Tools Setup Wizard, Why No Footstep? (window and in-game panel), Scene Overview, Help and Support.

Where to start #

  1. Quick start: footsteps on your own character in five minutes.
  2. Demo scenes: walk through a house, a garden and a guarded yard.
  3. Your controller and game: custom controllers, swimming and ladders, multiplayer, many NPCs.
  4. Why No Footstep?: when something sounds wrong.

Compatibility #

Unity 6000.0.85f1 and newer Unity 6 versions. Built-in, URP and HDRP. Both input systems (Responsive FX itself reads no input except the debugger's key). No package dependencies.
