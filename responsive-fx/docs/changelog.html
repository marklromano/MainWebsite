Changelog

[1.0.0] #

First release.

  • Impact Lab: surfaces against contact kinds in one grid, pairings (a blade on wood), the Event Trace with reasons, test buttons in Edit Mode and Play Mode, player option rules.
  • Surfaces and responses: 17 sample surfaces with answers for steps, landings, hits, bullets, bumps, doors, slides, rolls and breaks; weather conditions (wet, snowy) and footwear.
  • Hit feedback: numbers, flash, hitstop, shake, knockback, squash, health bars, sounds, sparks and the kill moment; genre presets, a Juice slider, Hit Recipe Studio; four looks that restyle numbers, bars, flash and sparks together.
  • Footsteps: any character, 2D and 3D, any controller; sounds, effects and footprints per surface. Character types (two legs, four legs, hopping, many legs, wheels, slither, hover) with their own rhythm, sound and marks.
  • Feedback Player: a list of feedbacks for any moment (a pickup, a jump, a button, a blast). 31 kinds of feedback, 8 ready moments, a no-code Feedback Trigger, saved Feedback Sequences that hits, surfaces and the Response Graph can play, and your own feedbacks in a few lines.
  • Contacts: physics objects answer their own bumps, slides and rolls; one call for bullets and anything else.
  • Noise and hearing: listeners for AI, rooms and doors, trails AI can follow.
  • Player screens: an options screen, captions, the Sound Ring, the noise meter, damage direction and low-health cues, all customisable and gated by your options asset.
  • Response Graph for special cases; bridges for Feel, Cinemachine, the Input System and TextMesh Pro.
  • Demos: the Response Playground, a combat arena, feature galleries and 2D scenes for hits and footsteps.
