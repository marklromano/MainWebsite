Make it yours

Everything a player sees, hears or feels can be changed or switched off, without code.

Start from a preset #

Eight presets change every part at once (see Profiles). Then the Juice slider for more or less of everything. The demos' Make it yours panel (M) switches presets and the main choices while you play.

Every part can be switched off #

In the profile each part has its own switch (Numbers, Flash, Hitstop mode, Shake, Knockback, Squash, Bar visibility, Sound, Particles, Kill moment). Per object, Overrides turns any part on or off for that object only, and gives it its own flash colour, number colour and size, and floating bar style (a boss with a wide gold bar and big gold numbers, minions with the default).

The look #

What Where
Number style in one click: Classic, Arcade Punch, Fantasy Relic, Tactical Clean Profile > Number style buttons
Numbers: Display as, motion, size, font, bold, outline, shadow, shine, gradient, critical burst and lean, format Profile > Numbers
When each part of a hit starts (normal, critical, kill) Hit Recipe Studio
Each kind's colour, colour-blind colour, word, suffix, size, icon Profile > Kinds
Where numbers appear: above the target, at the hit point, the middle, an offset, your own anchor Profile > Numbers, Hit Feedback > Number and bar anchor
Flash mode, colour, blinks Profile > Flash, Overrides > Flash Color
One character's numbers and bar Overrides > Number Color, Number Scale, Override Health Bar + Health Bar Style
Bar colours, size, rounded ends, segments, drain Profile > Floating health bar, Screen Health Bar
Vignette, arrow and pulse colours Player Hit Feedback
Sounds and particles Profile > Kinds (clips), Profile > Particles (your own prefab)

The profile inspector shows a live preview of the numbers as you change them.

Your own text and UI #

  • Your own font: drag any font asset into Profile > Numbers > Font.
  • Your TextMesh Pro font: add TextMesh Pro Numbers and drag in your TMP font asset.
  • Your own number display (UI Toolkit, your prefab): DamageNumbers.CustomDisplay.
  • Your own bars and HUD: read health from IHealthSource, react to HitFeedback.AnyHit, OnHit, OnKilled, and set Responsive FX's bars to Never.
  • Your own words in any language: HitFeedbackSettings.Translate.

Left as they are on purpose #

The Why No Feedback? window's look (an editor tool, not seen by players) and the demo panels (examples only).
